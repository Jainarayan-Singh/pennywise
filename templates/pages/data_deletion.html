You can permanently delete your {{ app_name }} account yourself, at any time.

How

  • Sign in and open Account.
  • Choose Delete account, type your account email to confirm and select Delete permanently.

What is removed

Immediately and permanently:

  • Your account, name, email address, password and passkeys.
  • All your transactions, categories and accounts.
  • Your sessions, pending sign-in links and Quick add drafts.
  • Your security log entries.

What remains

  • A single log entry that an account was deleted, with no personal details.
  • Copies in backups kept by the database host, until they expire under that host's own schedule.
  • Emails already sent to you, which stay in your own mailbox.

Cannot sign in?

Use Forgot password on the sign-in page to regain access, then delete the account.{% if contact_email %} If that does not work, write to {{ contact_email }} from the email address on the account.{% endif %}

Deletion cannot be undone.
